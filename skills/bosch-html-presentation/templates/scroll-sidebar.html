02 / CONTEXT

Start with the problem.

Explain what the audience needs to understand before introducing the solution. Ground the story in the current situation and one concrete challenge.

Where we are

Describe the current process, its users, and the constraints that shape the work.

What needs to change

Identify the friction that matters most and the outcome the team wants to achieve.

One clear problem gives every design decision a purpose.

03 / APPROACH

Build a shared understanding.

Introduce the solution in layers. Establish the overall idea, explain the key components, then show how they connect.

Explain the structure

Use a concise diagram or comparison when the relationships are easier to see than to describe.

Show the evidence

Use verified observations and label assumptions. Connect each result to the question it answers.

04 / WORKFLOW

Move through the process.

  1. Define the input

    Describe the starting point and the information available.

  2. Explain the transformation

    Show the decisions, actions, and boundaries involved.

  3. Inspect the outcome

    Make the result visible and explain how success is evaluated.

05 / TAKEAWAYS

Leave a clear next step.

Close with the central message and the action it supports. Keep conclusions proportional to the evidence presented.

Next decision

State what the audience should decide, investigate, or try next.
